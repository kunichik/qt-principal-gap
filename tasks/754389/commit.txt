Fix data race on a sender's vptr when its receiver is destroyed

~QObject() resolves the signal and calls disconnectNotify() on each of
its senders. Both are virtual calls, so they are only safe from the
thread that owns the sender: any other thread may be running that
sender's destructors right now, which rewrite its vptr as they go.
ThreadSanitizer reports this for any two connected objects destroyed in
parallel, and QtNetwork hits it on every HTTP request.

Keep notifying inline when the sender belongs to this thread, and post
the notification to the sender's own thread otherwise, unless that thread
can no longer deliver events (e.g. it has finished). If the sender is
indeed being destroyed, ~QObjectPrivate() discards the event.
