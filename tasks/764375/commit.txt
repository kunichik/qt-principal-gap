QtConcurrent: finish futures of tasks dropped by QThreadPool::clear()

QThreadPool::clear() removes queued runnables before they run. For a
QtConcurrent::run() task this leaves the associated future running
forever, so waitForFinished() never returns.

Report the future as canceled and finished when the underlying runnable
is deleted without having run.
