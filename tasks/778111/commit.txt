QByteArray: Don't preallocate the whole size hint in qUncompress()

qUncompress() preallocates the size declared in the 4-byte big-endian
header of the data. The header comes from the input, so a ~10-byte blob
declaring 0xFFFFFFFF triggered a ~4 GiB allocation.

The declared size is only a hint, so don't reject such data. Instead,
limit the preallocation to what deflate can produce out of the given
amount of compressed data (its maximum compression ratio is ~1032:1).
The output buffer still grows as needed, so data with a wrong hint
decompresses as before.
