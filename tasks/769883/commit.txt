QDate: Reject trailing data in Qt::ISODate parsing

A valid ISO date is always exactly 10 characters with fixed separators.
QDate::fromString(s, Qt::ISODate) only required the string to be at
least 10 characters and that the character at index 10, if any, was not
a digit. As a result "2024-01-02 anything at all" with a trailing part
parsed to a valid QDate, silently discarding the tail. This was
inconsistent with the QDateTime overload and Qt::TextDate path, so
require the string to be exactly "yyyy-MM-dd" and reject any trailing
data.
Document that ISODate requires exactly "yyyy-MM-dd" and that its field
separators are lenient: any punctuation character is accepted in place
of '-'.

[ChangeLog][QtCore][QDate] QDate::fromString() with Qt::ISODate or
Qt::ISODateWithMs now rejects trailing data after the "yyyy-MM-dd" date.
Previously a string such as "2024-01-02 ..." parsed to a valid date and
the trailing text was silently ignored; it now returns an invalid QDate,
consistent with other handling of Qt::TextDate formats.
