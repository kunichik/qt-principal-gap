QByteArray: support constexpr construction from literals

The _ba literal and QByteArrayLiteral() already point at static byte
data, but the QByteArray wrapper could not be created during constant
evaluation.

Reuse the constexpr-capable QArrayDataPointer path and make QByteArray
destruction, move operations, and swapping constexpr when supported.
Make the DataPointer constructor and _ba literal operator constexpr in
those builds.
