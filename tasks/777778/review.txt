At the Qt level I want a solution that works for non-KDE Qt apps that don't use DBus, too.

The mtime check is what other technology stacks do too, and my experience says it's far more reliable than any kind of DBus-signal/file-watching over-engineering (remember ksycoca not reliably updating in kde3/kde4, while now it does a much better job at keeping up-to-date because it does a mtime check instead of relying on file-watching+DBus).
