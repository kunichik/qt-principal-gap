Names of Gerrit reviewers in review texts, judge notes and audits are replaced by stable aliases
(Reviewer 01..18), so the analysis reads as being about the code, not the people.

This is pseudonymisation, not anonymity: the reviews are public on codereview.qt-project.org, and
the commit messages in tasks/*/commit.txt are kept verbatim as in qtbase, including their
Reviewed-by: lines, from which aliases can be matched to names.
